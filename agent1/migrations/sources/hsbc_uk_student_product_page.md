## Who can apply

- You can apply for a Student Bank Account if you are 18 or over.
- You live in the UK and have lived in the UK, Channel Islands or Isle of Man for the past 3 years.
- You have proof you've been accepted onto a qualifying course (e.g. your 16-digit UCAS code).
- If you're returning to do a postgraduate or secondary degree of 1 year or more at a UK university, you must have completed an undergraduate course in the last 3 years.
- If you've been living in the UK, Channel Islands or Isle of Man for less than 3 years, please apply for an HSBC Bank Account instead.

## Proof you've been accepted on a course

- For online application you need a 16-digit UCAS status code, received in an email from UCAS.
- Alternative forms of proof (for other channels or if you don't have the UCAS code): a letter from your university or further education provider confirming your acceptance of a place on a qualifying course — dated within the last 4 months and on official headed paper (paper or PDF); or a print-out showing your UCAS status from the UCAS Track website.
- If you use one of these alternative forms of proof, it may take longer to open your account.

## Qualifying courses

- Your course must be full-time (or part-time if you'll be receiving a Disabled Students' Allowance) and at a UK university or college.
- Qualifying courses include: postgraduate degree (e.g. PhD, MBA, MSc, PGCE, MPhil, M.Ed, M.St or LL.M); undergraduate degree (e.g. BA, BSc, BEd, BN); Diploma of Higher Education (DipHE); BTEC; NVQ levels 4 and 5; foundation degree — FDA (art based) or FdSc (science based) and/or subsequent one year full honours degree course; pre-registration or First Level Nursing and Midwifery Diploma; a foundation year which is an integral part of one of the courses listed above (written proof required, e.g. a letter from the college or university).
- Courses that don't qualify include: apprenticeships (intermediate Level 2, advanced Level 3, higher Level 4, degree Levels 5-7); access and conversion courses; AVCE; non-apprenticeship standard City and Guilds and RSA courses; GCE A Levels or AS courses or Scottish Highers; BTEC National Awards (other than HND/HNC); GNVQ Levels 1-3; SQA Awards, GCSEs; traineeships / apprenticeship access courses; part-time courses (except for students receiving Disabled Students' Allowance).

## Fees, overdraft and savings features

- No monthly account fee.
- Interest-free arranged overdraft: up to £1,000 when you open the account (regardless of what year you're in), which could rise to £2,000 in year 2 and £3,000 in year 3, subject to status and how your account has been used.
- Representative example: 0% EAR (variable), representative 0% APR (variable), based on an arranged overdraft of £1,000.
- Overdrafts are designed for short-term borrowing only, and are subject to status. Account activity including overdraft usage is reported to credit reference agencies.
- Regular Saver at 5.00% AER / gross: save between £25 and £250 a month for a fixed 12-month term (withdrawal restrictions apply).
- App features: instant notifications, Spending insights and Monthly budgets, Balance forecast, view card PIN, freeze/unfreeze card, Apple Pay / Google Pay / Samsung Pay.

## Application details and FAQ

- You'll need details of your addresses over the past 3 years, your income, your university course, and your 16-digit UCAS status code. You may also need to provide ID, proof of your address and immigration status.
- New to HSBC: apply online in minutes. Already have an HSBC current account: apply online and HSBC will upgrade your existing account to a Student Account; any Direct Debits and standing orders will still be in place.
- You're normally only allowed to have one student bank account.
- The Student Bank Account automatically changes to a Graduate Account on 31 July of the year you finish studying.
- Eligible deposits with HSBC UK Bank plc are protected up to a total of £120,000, or up to £240,000 for joint accounts, by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS). This limit applies to the total of deposits held with HSBC UK, HSBC Private Bank, first direct, M&S Bank and M&S Savings and Investments.
