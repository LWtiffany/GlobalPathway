## Eligibility

You can apply for an HSBC Teens account if you:

- are new to HSBC;
- are aged 11 to 17;
- hold a valid Hong Kong Permanent Identity Card (HKID);
- have a Hong Kong birth certificate or a Medical Certificate of Birth of the People's Republic of China;
- are residing in and physically present in Hong Kong when you submit the application; and
- have a parent who is an existing HSBC customer with access to the HSBC HK App.

## Account features and fees

- Save and spend across 12 currencies.
- There are no monthly charges or minimum balance requirements.
- Daily transfer and withdrawal limits apply. Both the teen and parent receive real-time mobile transfer notifications.
- The teen can use FPS, make ATM withdrawals and use the Budget tool in the HSBC HK App.

## Mobile opening process

- Open the account with a parent in the HSBC HK App in about 15 minutes. The parent and teen each need a separate phone with the app installed.
- The parent starts HSBC Teens Account Opening, enters both parties' information, uploads the Hong Kong birth certificate as relationship proof and accepts the terms.
- The teen scans the QR code shown on the parent's phone, photographs the HKID, takes a selfie and accepts the terms.

## Upgrade at age 18

At age 18 the customer can complete an Integrated Account Conversion Form and upgrade based on their needs while keeping the account relationship. The product page explains that the account can grow into HSBC One with the same account number.
