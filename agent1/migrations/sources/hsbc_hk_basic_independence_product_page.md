## Product purpose

Basic Banking Account with Independence is intended to support everyday banking for a customer with diminished mental capacity while an authorised person helps oversee the customer's financial arrangements.

## Legal authority

HSBC's guide explains the Enduring Power of Attorney route: the donor signs an EPOA before a practising solicitor in Hong Kong while the donor still has mental capacity. If the donor subsequently loses mental capacity, the attorney registers the EPOA with the High Court. HSBC's product materials also describe other bank-accepted legal-authority routes, such as a Guardianship Order or court-appointed committee, subject to the bank's case review.

## How to open

The donor and attorney visit an HSBC branch. The bank reviews the case and the authority under which the supporting person will act.

## Documents

For the EPOA route, bring the registered EPOA and the attorney's identification document and proof of address. HSBC may request further information or documents depending on the authority and the individual circumstances.
