## Who can apply?

You can apply for a HSBC Premier Account if you meet any of the following:

### Total relationship balance

Maintain a Total Relationship Balance (TRB) with us of HKD1,000,000 or more. Below Balance Fee will be waived for first six months if your TRB falls below that amount.

TRB includes your savings, your investments and insurance, any retirement schemes you're a part of and utilised lending facilities.

### International Premier

Hold or qualify for HSBC Premier in another country or region.

If you are looking to open a sole account instead of a joint account, you can apply online within minutes using the following link.
