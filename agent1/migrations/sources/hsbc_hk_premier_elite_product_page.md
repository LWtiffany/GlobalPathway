## How to qualify

HSBC Premier Elite is a privileged and personalised service for clients who maintain a Total Relationship Balance of at least HKD7.8 million with HSBC in Hong Kong.

## Existing Premier relationship

The Premier Elite Terms and Conditions state that Premier Elite is available to holders of an HSBC Premier Bank Account who have maintained an average Total Relationship Balance in Hong Kong over the immediately preceding three consecutive calendar months of at least HKD7,800,000.

## Total Relationship Balance

TRB may include Hong Kong dollar, renminbi and foreign currency deposits; the market value of eligible investments; utilised lending facilities excluding mortgages and credit-card balances; qualifying life insurance; and eligible HSBC MPF or ORSO balances, subject to the product terms.

## Application

Premier Elite is a service tier above HSBC Premier. Customers should register through HSBC's Premier Elite channel or a Hong Kong branch and complete the bank's eligibility checks. It is not positioned as a general entry-level current account.
