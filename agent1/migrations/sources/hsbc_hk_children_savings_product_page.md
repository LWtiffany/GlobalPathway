## Eligibility and how to apply

- To apply, you need to be under the age of 18. No fees or minimum balance needed.
- To open an account, your parent or guardian needs to be present.
- Book an appointment: fill out the online form with a few personal details and HSBC will help you open the account at one of the branches. Children interested in the account should talk to their parents/guardian and ask them to book an appointment.
- Documents required are listed in the HSBC account application notes (PDF, see official page).

## For children under 11 years of age

- If you have a child under 11 years of age, you can set up an HSBC Children Savings Account in your name for your children and kick-start their financial education with this account.
- Review monthly statements with your children, introducing them to essential banking aspects such as deposits, withdrawals, savings and interest.
- Supervise your children's accounts 24/7 via online banking, mobile banking, ATMs and phone banking.
- Note (footnote 3): if your child is under 18 and unable to manage finances independently, you may choose to open an HSBC Children Savings Account (in re account) in your name for your children, and all transactions must be authorised with your signature(s).

## Account features

- Check your account balance in the HSBC HK App, with push notifications for account activities.
- Mobile cash withdrawal: withdraw cash at any local HSBC and Hang Seng ATM using the HSBC HK App.
- HSBC HK App Lite mode: simple to use, easy to learn — switch from Full Mode to Lite Mode for a stress-free start.
- PayMe: download PayMe and register with HKID and personal details to pay family and friends, split bills, and grab vouchers.
- FPS (Faster Payment System): send and receive money via the HSBC HK App using mobile number, email address, FPS identifier or QR code — transfers are instant and free of charge from HSBC (other banks may have different charging policies).
- 'Budget' on the HSBC HK App: keep track of spending across categories with personalised insights.
- HSBC Mastercard Debit Card Supplementary Card for spending abroad with access to 12 major currencies at zero fees (discuss with parent/guardian).
- Turning 18: unlock HSBC One as the first adult account — digital-first, all-in-one, no minimum balance required.
