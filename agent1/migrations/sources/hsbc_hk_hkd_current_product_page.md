## Product purpose

Hong Kong Dollar Current Account is a straightforward day-to-day banking account. It is a simple, convenient account that can issue paper cheques for day-to-day payments.

## How to apply

The product page instructs applicants to visit a branch with the required documents.

## Identity and supporting information

The official account application notes state that adult Hong Kong permanent residents provide a Hong Kong identity card. Non-permanent or non-Hong Kong residents provide an accepted HKID or valid travel document. HSBC may request current residential-address evidence, supplementary documents and information about the purpose of the account, source of income or wealth, anticipated transactions, opening funds and tax residence.

## Difference from savings accounts

HSBC's account FAQ explains that a savings account allows customers to earn interest while retaining access to funds, whereas a current account is used to manage money and write cheques for day-to-day payments.
