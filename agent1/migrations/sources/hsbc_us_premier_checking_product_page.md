## Who can apply

- To apply for a Premier checking account, you need to be at least 18 years old and located in one of the designated countries (list subject to change).
- Already an HSBC U.S. client: apply through your online banking account. Others: apply online; joint accounts or applicants not eligible for the online channel can request a call back.

## Monthly maintenance fee and waiver paths

- A monthly maintenance fee of $50 will be incurred if at least one of the following HSBC Premier requirements is not maintained (full details in the 'HSBC Premier Terms and Charges Disclosure'):
  - Balances of $100,000 in combined U.S. consumer and qualifying commercial U.S. Dollar deposit and investment accounts; OR
  - Monthly recurring direct deposits totaling at least $5,000 from a third party to HSBC Premier checking account(s); OR
  - Any HSBC U.S. residential mortgage loan serviced by HSBC (Home Equity products, loans in foreclosure or bankruptcy, and loans for which servicing rights have been transferred are not included); OR
  - Consumers who maintain Private Bank status (to become an HSBC Private Bank customer, you must be invited and reviewed on an individual basis).
- Qualifying direct deposits are electronic deposits of regular periodic payments (such as salary, pension, Social Security, or other regular monthly income) deposited through the ACH network by your employer or an outside agency. Transfers from one account to another, mobile deposits, or deposits made at a Wealth Center or ATM do not qualify.

## Waiver Period and account closure rule

- You will not be assessed a Monthly Maintenance Fee for the calendar month in which you open your account and the following 3 calendar months (together, the "Waiver Period"), whether you meet the qualifying criteria or not.
- If immediately after the Waiver Period you have not met one of the HSBC Premier qualification criteria and your HSBC Premier checking account is charged 3 consecutive Monthly Maintenance Fees, your account will be closed.
- In addition, any other checking, savings, credit card and Global Money Accounts that you own will also be closed, unless you (or a joint account holder on those accounts) hold a different HSBC Premier checking account.

## New customer bonus

- For a limited time, earn up to $3,000 when you open a new Premier checking account and complete qualifying activities. Offer ends December 31, 2026.
