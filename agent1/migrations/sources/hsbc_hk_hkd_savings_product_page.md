## Product purpose

Use your savings flexibly for transactions and withdrawals, and earn interest on your balance.

- Access money 24 hours a day using an ATM card, phone banking or online banking.
- Detailed monthly statements are available for Integrated Account and Statement Savings Account customers.
- Interest rates are variable; the latest deposit rates are published separately by HSBC.

## How to apply

Visit a branch with the required documents. The official account application notes explain that adult applicants provide an accepted identity document. HSBC may request residential-address evidence and supplementary documents depending on residence, account type, local rules and the individual case.

## Information the bank may request

HSBC may ask for nationality, occupation and employer, account purpose, reason for opening an account in Hong Kong, source of wealth or income, anticipated transaction activity, source of opening funds, services to be used, tax residence and taxpayer identification information.

## Difference from current and time deposit accounts

A savings account earns interest while allowing access to funds. A current account is designed for day-to-day payments and cheque writing. A time deposit fixes money for a term and early withdrawal is subject to the bank's discretion and may lose interest or incur a charge.
