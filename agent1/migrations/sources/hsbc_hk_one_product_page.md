## Account opening channels and process

- Channels: HSBC HK App (primary), branch, Digital Banker (at designated Express Banking locations).
- The HSBC HK App account opening takes about 5 minutes, in 4 steps; actual time may vary.
- Customers who choose instant ATM card collection can get the card immediately after successful account opening.
- Digital Banker account opening is applicable to customers aged 18-75 who currently hold no HSBC bank/investment accounts or credit cards.

## Eligibility, balance and fees

- HKID holders: no minimum Total Relationship Balance (TRB) requirement; no Below Balance Fee.
- Non-HKID holders who open on or after 1 January 2026: maintain an average TRB of HKD10,000 (for 3 consecutive months) to waive the HKD100 monthly Below Balance Fee.
- Non-HKID customers: lower transfer and withdrawal limits during the first 6 months after account opening.
- Non-HKID supported passports for app/Digital Banker onboarding: Australia, Canada, India, Singapore, the United Kingdom, the United States.
- Digital Banker: aged 18-75, currently holding no HSBC bank/investment accounts or credit cards.

## Account features

- 12 major currencies supported (HKD + 11 foreign currencies: USD, GBP, JPY, CNY, EUR, THB, AUD, NZD, SGD, CAD, CHF).
- 24/7 FX exchange in the HSBC HK App, with FX Order Watch and rate alerts.
- Daily banking: salary auto-payroll setup, time deposits, 'Budget' expense tracking and monthly budgeting across HSBC accounts and credit cards, Future Planner financial planning, 'Chat with us' for wealth/investment questions.
- Investment: HSBC Trade25 (HK/US/A-share trading, lifetime $0 commission, HKD25 per month only in months with trades or holdings); FlexInvest funds from HKD100.

## Welcome offers (promotion period 2026-09-01 to 2026-12-31, selected items)

- New HSBC One customers with qualifying new funds: up to HKD3,530 RewardCash (no registration needed).
- Salary-related: new Auto-Payroll customers can receive up to HKD1,500 RewardCash plus up to HKD600 lifestyle offers; eligible payroll customers may join a business-class travel lucky draw.
- FX offer: spend designated FX amounts to earn a 20% spread-discount FX coupon.
