## Hong Kong Dollar Time Deposit

- Start saving with HKD10,000.
- Choose a term from 1 day to 36 months.
- Manage the time deposit through branches, phone banking, Mobile Banking and Online Banking.
- HSBC Premier customers may receive a preferential interest rate.

## Renminbi and foreign-currency minimums

- Renminbi Time Deposit starts from RMB10,000, with terms from one week to one year.
- Foreign Currency Time Deposit may start from 2,000 units; Japanese yen and Thai baht require 100,000 units, and Swiss franc and Singapore dollar require 10,000 units. Available currencies and thresholds are subject to the current page.

## How to place a time deposit

Existing time-deposit, HSBC Premier or HSBC One customers can log on to the HSBC HK App or Online Banking to place a deposit. A customer without an HSBC bank account or active time deposit should first open or upgrade to HSBC Premier or HSBC One; a time deposit can then be placed on the next business day after the account is successfully opened.

## Early withdrawal

Early withdrawal may be allowed only at the bank's discretion. HSBC may pay no interest and may charge for the early withdrawal. Principal is normally returned on the maturity date.
